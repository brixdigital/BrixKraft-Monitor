# BrixKraft Monitor Privacy Policy

BrixKraft Monitor shows your PC's network speed and system usage on your taskbar.

**What stays on your PC.** Your settings, daily data usage totals and internet outage history are saved
only on your PC. They are removed when you uninstall the app.

**What leaves your PC.** BrixKraft has no servers for this app, no account system and no analytics.
The app makes only these connections:

- A ping every 5 seconds to the address in Settings (default 1.1.1.1, operated by Cloudflare), to measure
  delay and detect outages. You can change the address or turn this off.
- A web request every 60 seconds to each status light address you add yourself.

Those services can see your IP address, as with any connection you make.

**What the app reads.** It reads network totals, CPU, memory, GPU and disk usage, and the names and
resource use of running apps, to display them to you. None of this is sent anywhere.

**Purchases.** Any purchase is handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement.
